Look

I keep a knowledge graph of what matters to you: people, projects, preferences, plans and how they change over time. It's stored only on this computer (~/.jarvis/graph.db). Open the Memory graph view to explore it; click a node to see or forget it.

Nightly clean-up

Once a night I write a short diary of the day, merge duplicate people and facts, and let facts nobody has used in a long time fade into history, so memory stays sharp over the years. Names, family, birthdays, home and work never fade. If the PC is off at that time, I catch up when it's next on.

Then just ask: “what did that contract say about payment?” I'll tell you which file and page the answer came from. Everything stays on this computer (~/.jarvis).

Briefings

Briefings cover weather, your calendar and email (when connected under Connections), reminders and what I remember. Ask me “remind me at 6…” or “every Monday, clean my Downloads” to schedule things.

Ambient mode

I only look at the active window's title and whether you've touched the keyboard or mouse. I stay quiet during meetings, while you're away, and while I'm busy.

Screen awareness

Uses screenshots when the model can see images, otherwise only window titles. Nothing is stored. With Claude, a smaller model such as claude-haiku-5-5 keeps glances cheap.

Screen HUD

A transparent, Iron Man style layer over your screens. It locks on to whatever I click, sweeps the screen when I look at it, points to buttons when I'm showing you how to do something (“show me how to export this”), and keeps live stats in the corner. It never blocks your mouse, and it's hidden from screenshots and screen sharing.

Hand gestures

Control me with your webcam, no special hardware. The camera picture never leaves this PC and nothing is recorded: each frame becomes 21 hand points and is thrown away. The first start downloads a small hand-tracking model (7.5 MB).

At the desk

I greet you when you sit down, lock the PC when you walk away, and tell you when someone else sits down. I use the same webcam stream as hand gestures (camera number under Desktop). Your face is kept only as a list of numbers in ~/.jarvis/presence on this PC, never as a photo, and nothing about faces is ever sent anywhere. The first start downloads two small face models (37 MB).

Health nudges

Tell me “I drank some water” and I'll count it.

Weekly life report

Once a minute I note which app is in front (its name and a category like coding or meetings, never the window title), stored in ~/.jarvis/life.db on this PC. Each week I read you a short report: where the time went, your goals and habits, water, posture and sleep. Set goals by asking, e.g. “I want to exercise 3 times a week” or “code at most 40 hours a week”.

Then just say “turn the bedroom AC to 24” or “TV off”.
